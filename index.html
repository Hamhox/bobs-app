Who is Bob, and what is this?

I make the missing thing.

Bob spent most of his career inside one product company, where the gaps between design, engineering, product data, content, and operations kept becoming his job. When the team needed a prototype, an internal tool, a product interface, a launch system, or a clearer way to understand a product, he built the missing thing.

Bob's App is the public home for that work: a guided portfolio, working applications, and a growing collection of the artifacts around them. Start with the portfolio for the complete story, or open a project directly.
