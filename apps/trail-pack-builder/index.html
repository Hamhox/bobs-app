About this data

Source geometry is cleaned. Prebuilt packs omit elevation and device telemetry. At one point, all trails were probably on public land, but it is up to you to make sure you are riding responsibly at this late date. Do not follow a GPS device's advice into the middle of a lake.

Quick start

Tap a named riding area, then choose Download GPX.

Each riding-area file is prebuilt and checked against the supported GPS capacity limits.

Desktop shortcuts: Space — pan · Z — zoom (hold Alt to zoom out) · Ctrl+0 — fit all trails · Ctrl+1 — fit the selected area · Ctrl++ / Ctrl+− — zoom now
